# Concepts for video #1 (CP2)

Three original scenes built with the realism pass: 4× textures, normal maps, bevelled edges, leaf-card foliage, environment reflections, SSAA 2×. Render with `node src/cli.mjs still scenes/<name>`. Stills are in `out/concepts/` (gitignored).

## A. Autumn Treetop Library — `scenes/treetop`
- **Pitch:** a reading room high in a maple. Its open balcony looks out over a rainy autumn canopy village: lantern-lit treehouses, a rope bridge from our balcony to a neighbor's, and string lights between the trees.
- **Palette:** amber interior; orange/red foliage; blue-violet storm sky.
- **Signature:** the outside comes in. Vines hang over the opening, leaves gather on the balcony and rug, and a leaf drifts in now and then.
- **Motion:** rain in 3 depth layers; fire; steam from a mug; drifting leaves. Later: lantern sway, lightning far off, someone crossing the bridge (rare).
- **Creature:** gray tabby cat curled on a cushion by the hearth.
- **Caution:** this is the closest of the three to the user's treehouse reference (fire, treehouses, bridges). The room, opening and layout are different.

## B. Lakeside Stilt Cabin — `scenes/lakeside`
- **Pitch:** a writing room on stilts over a rainy lake at blue hour. A wide multi-pane window faces a village of lit cottages under pine hills, and their lights stretch across the water.
- **Palette:** honey-wood interior; slate blue and grey-green exterior; warm village pinpoints.
- **Signature:** reflections on the lake, with rain rings and long streaks of warm light.
- **Motion:** rain; drops beading and running down the glass; ripple shimmer; stove glow flicker. Later: a boat passing with a lantern (rare), village windows going dark through the night.
- **Creature:** chestnut-and-white spaniel asleep by the cast-iron stove.

## C. Canal Bookshop Loft — `scenes/loft`
- **Pitch:** the loft above a bookshop. A wall-wide steel factory window looks over a rainy canal: gabled townhouses with lit windows and shopfronts, gold street trees, lamps, an arched footbridge, moored houseboats.
- **Palette:** brick red and amber inside; dark plum street with gold trees and warm lights.
- **Signature:** a long window seat under string lights; the canal reflects the street.
- **Motion:** rain; drops on the glass; leaves falling from the street trees; steam; candle flicker. Later: a cyclist with a light crossing the bridge (rare), a boat light passing.
- **Creature:** dark tabby cat curled on the window seat.
- **This is the close cousin** of the original rainy-apartment reference (city at night through a big window). The layout and setting are original.

## Recommendation
**B (Lakeside)** for video #1. It has the most distinct identity of the three, the reflections give lots of calm motion, it sits furthest from every reference, and its layout leaves room for rare events. A and C make good video #2 and #3.
