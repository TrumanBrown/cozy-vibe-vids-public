# Agent guide — cozy-vibe-vids

10-hour cozy ambience videos for YouTube, in many aesthetics (Minecraft, wizard libraries, rainy cafés, Ghibli-like cottages, ramen alleys, pixel art; [config/aesthetics.json](config/aesthetics.json)). Each scene is **one AI key-art image** (Azure gpt-image), animated in code (rain, drips, rain on glass and on the ground, snow, fire, steam, candles, neon, traffic, rivers, mist, foliage, lanterns, birds, a crawling critter, and the pet's breathing) and rendered deterministically to 4K. **Read [docs/STATUS.md](docs/STATUS.md) first, then [docs/GOALS.md](docs/GOALS.md) (spec) and [docs/SETUP.md](docs/SETUP.md) (machine setup). Update STATUS.md before you stop.**

Custom agents: **Video Studio** ([.github/agents/video-studio.agent.md](.github/agents/video-studio.agent.md)) makes and fixes videos; **Media and Outreach** ([.github/agents/media-outreach.agent.md](.github/agents/media-outreach.agent.md)) drafts promotion.

**A new video starts from one line** (user 2026-10-21): "generate me a new youtube video sample for <idea>", or `/new-sample <idea>`. `tools/autopilot.py` takes it to a 5 minute sample with music and reviews it itself (gates: review.py, critic.py); the agent fixes what the gates find and stops once, for Truman's OK. Then `tools/finish_video.py`. How it is built and why: [docs/research-agent-architecture.md](docs/research-agent-architecture.md); his settled defaults: [config/defaults.json](config/defaults.json).

Ideas for later that need new code first (critter town: our own creatures and kids walking a top-down pixel village): [docs/IDEAS.md](docs/IDEAS.md).

## Non-negotiables

- **Provenance is logged.** Every external or AI-generated asset used in a video goes in [docs/asset-ledger.csv](docs/asset-ledger.csv) *before* use (source, model/author, license, date). Third-party assets must be CC0 unless the user approves otherwise; SIL Open Font License fonts for title text are approved (user 2026-10-20, [config/fonts.json](config/fonts.json)). User-supplied music is logged too.
- **Minecraft look is allowed (user decision 2026-10-15).** Prompts may say "Minecraft" and the game's block style, lanterns, chests and cats are OK. Still never use Mojang logos or brand text in renders. In titles and channel names "Minecraft" may only be a secondary part; `credits.txt` carries Mojang's not-official disclaimer.
- **Other franchise looks are treated like Minecraft (user decision 2026-10-16).** Hogwarts-like, Ghibli-like, Stardew-like, Lofi Girl-like rooms: named in prompts, only a secondary part of titles, never logos or brand text in renders.
- **No people in key art (user 2026-10-16):** humans read as AI. Pets and animals are fine, and so is the channel's own cast of made-up creatures ([config/cast.json](config/cast.json)). The aesthetics boilerplate says so in every prompt.
- **Key art must not read as AI slop (user 2026-10-25).** He found the first key art far too generic and AI-made, and wants the level of detail of the hand-made art he likes. His references are analyzed in the keyart-pipeline skill's [references/art-direction.md](.agents/skills/keyart-pipeline/references/art-direction.md): one visible medium, a chosen palette, light from a real source with darkness allowed, detail only where the eye goes, a small story that makes sense, our own creatures. The approved hand-made styles are the looks `ink-gouache` and `brushwork-night` (gpt-image-2); a new look is tested on a few images and he picks before anything else runs on it.
- **Minecraft music: only the Mojang-owned allowlist (user decision 2026-10-16).**
  - Only the tracks in [config/minecraft_music.json](config/minecraft_music.json), imported from the user's game files by `tools/mc_music.py`. They are Mojang-owned tracks by its hired composers, on the official Creator-Safe Playlist, and chill.
  - Never C418 (third parties still file Content ID claims), never "Lava Chicken", and nothing outside the list.
  - Run `mc_music.py check` before each new video.
  - Upload as Private and publish only after YouTube's copyright check is clear (DECISIONS 2026-10-16 "Minecraft music").
- **Public text never mentions AI, and stays true (user decision 2026-10-16).**
  - Credits, titles, descriptions and the channel page leave AI out, but never claim hand-drawn art, stock-library sources or "no AI".
  - YouTube's "AI use" answer is No for stylized scenes; for photoreal scenes `config/aesthetics.json` suggests Yes and the user decides at upload (2026-10-16: photoreal is in, the label isn't a big concern). AI-made music in the playlist (open-lofi tracks are Suno) makes it Yes: YouTube lists "AI generated music" as needing disclosure (the ramen alley, user 2026-10-20).
  - Internal records (ledger, sidecars, docs) keep full provenance.
- **Promotion is drafted, never posted by an agent (DECISIONS 2026-10-16).** The media and outreach agent (`.github/agents/media-outreach.agent.md`) drafts and logs. Truman posts, comments and messages himself. No bots or scheduled posting, no comments on other creators' videos that send their viewers here, one post per place per video, and each place's rules are checked and logged first.
- **Public text is written in Truman's voice (user 2026-10-16), the way he publishes it (user 2026-10-20).** Draft titles and descriptions from what he actually published: `docs/records/<slug>/published.md` and master-render "Upload checklist" step 4. Summary:
  - The title is "Lofi Ramen Alley. Study and relax with calming rain. 4K, 10 hours, no Ads": the music and the place's name, the use, the specs. No emoji since 2026-10-26; the ramen alley went out as "Lofi Ramen Alley 🍜 Study and relax with calming rain ☔ ...".
  - The description opens with "Come relax with rain on ...", then "10 hours of 4K ... Never interrupting with ads." A video with the channel's own creatures names their species there (config/cast.json `species`; user 2026-10-26).
  - Then his blurb (`assets/publish/description_blurb.txt`, first person), credits only where a track asks for them, and about 8 hashtags. No moments list, tags optional, category Film & Animation.
  - Still no dashes, exclamation marks or stock AI words, and nothing he or the timeline can't back up. No emoji anywhere, titles included (a spec from before keeps its titles' with `"title_emoji": 2`). `tools/upload_package.py` refuses the checkable part.
  - Once a video is live, record what he changed (step 9 there).
- **Titles and thumbnails say 4K and the length (user 2026-10-16).** Every title says 4K. Every thumbnail carries the spec's `badges`: "4K" and the length ("10 HR"). The suggested thumbnail and the Test & compare trio must have both, and `tools/upload_package.py` refuses the package otherwise. "NO ADS" is Treetop's choice (ads can come later); it isn't required. Details: master-render "Thumbnails" and DECISIONS 2026-10-16.
- **Thumbnails fit the video's look (user 2026-10-20).** Each look's thumbnails use its own fonts and motifs (`config/aesthetics.json` `thumb`): neon, signs, paper and handwriting for the ramen alley. Take liberties, and make several different concepts per video. Also offer the channel's pixel-font concept: a big hero (pet or prop) with the place name and the music tag. That's what he picked on both videos (Treetop `place-card-cat`, the ramen alley `pixel-bowl`), so suggest it first. upload_package wants at least 3 look-styled concepts for any look that isn't pixel art. A look painted by hand (`thumb.painterly`, user 2026-10-26: a nature look in a pastel, painted style needs thumbnails in the same style) gets no pixel letters at all: pastel paper concepts, each its own medium with its own words (a field guide plate labeling the species, a watercolor wash, a nature journal, a storybook cover, a specimen label).
- **Monetization-safe variety.** YouTube's *inauthentic content* policy targets template-made, interchangeable videos, and "a still image plus overlays" is the risky pattern. Every video needs distinct key art and rich, varied motion and events, never just a palette swap.
- **Viewer comfort.** No strobing: lightning is a soft ramped glow, at most 3 flashes per second (WCAG 2.3.1). No sudden loud audio. Locked camera.
- **Deterministic.** All randomness is seeded; time = `frameIndex / fps`, never wall-clock. Same config + seed gives identical frames, events and audio. (AI key art is generated once and saved; it is never regenerated during a render.)
- **Seamless.** No visible pops or seams at loop/clip/segment boundaries, no flicker, no audio clicks. Animations use integer cycles per loop.
- **Nothing that moves in reality sits frozen (user 2026-10-16, every video).** Painted steam, smoke, flames and hanging drops are removed from the key art and animated; rain lands on visible ground and water (also between a stool's legs or a railing's balusters: never cut a box around a near object); rivers flow; birds, a small creature, window life and mist keep the background alive. new-video skill "The standing quality bar"; `motion_review.py --static`, `weather_audit.py` and `outdoor_check.py` (nothing inside under the weather) check it.
- **Every revision is remembered (user 2026-10-19).** Each change Truman has asked for is a row in [lessons.md](.agents/skills/new-video/references/lessons.md) and is applied on the first render; `tools/review.py` runs the checks and must PASS before he sees anything. A new request gets a row the same round. Each video also gets a signature detail (brief `signature_detail`), like the ramen alley's ant.
- **A pet painted in the key art only breathes (user 2026-10-18, every video).** Generated video of the painted pet (lifting its head, looking around, sitting up) looked eerie, jerky, unclear and obviously AI, so it is never used in any style. A painted pet gets breathing and an occasional sigh, nothing more (no ear flicks or tail twitches), and is painted asleep or resting. A pet that really moves must be a fully rendered 3D model or a library asset (rigged model, sprite sheet) animated in code, never stitched generated frames, and only in looks it matches (blocky, pixel art, low-poly). Assets are free: CC0, nothing paid; CC-BY only with Truman's OK. DECISIONS 2026-10-18. **The channel's own cast (user 2026-10-25)** may be painted awake doing something quiet (reading, warming up at a fire), with breathing and blinks drawn in code; their silly touches (a lantern, a scarf, plants growing on them) move with the wind in code. Still never generated video of them.
- **Motion must look real**: physically motivated, irregular/clumpy, layered in depth, lit by the scene, and never passing through solid geometry. See the [motion quality bar](docs/REFERENCE.md#motion-quality-bar-user-feedback-2026-10-15--applies-to-all-animations) before building any animation.

## Working agreements

- **Ask before** spending money beyond small image batches, creating new cloud resources outside `rg-cozyvibevids-img`, adding non-CC0 third-party assets, installing third-party skills or packages that aren't vetted, or deleting user files.
- **Azure is sandboxed:** only touch resource group `rg-cozyvibevids-img` (see [config/azure.json](config/azure.json)). Auth is keyless (`az login`); never create or commit API keys.
- **Vision spend (user 2026-10-22):** the rain check's first passes run on a local model (llama.cpp on this PC's GPU, [config/local_vision.json](config/local_vision.json)) and Azure confirms what it flags and has the final look; nothing else is decided by the local model without Truman's OK. Lessons L122; setup in [docs/SETUP.md](docs/SETUP.md) section 7.
- **Checkpoints:** the original phase checkpoints in [GOALS.md](docs/GOALS.md#roadmap--checkpoints) are done. For a new video there is one stop: Truman's OK on the autopilot sample (new-video skill). Stop there and wait.
- **Look before you show.** View renders, crops and diffs yourself before presenting anything.
- **Answer him in the chat (user 2026-10-23).** When Truman asks something, explain it plainly and in full in the final message he reads (the task-complete summary, if that's what closes the turn). STATUS.md and DECISIONS.md keep a copy for the next agent, never instead of the answer.
- **Sound first, then build once (user 2026-10-16).** Audio changes (levels, music, opening) are judged on short clips (`tools/loop_master.py --preview`, `node src/cli.mjs preview`) and approved before any 10 h file is rebuilt. Approval means the user has listened to the exact final settings and said so. The autopilot sample carries the final music and ambience settings, so his OK on it counts; any sound change after that goes back to him as a clip. Choosing options in a form is not approval: make clips of the choice, then wait.
- **Strong openings (user 2026-10-16).** The first minutes get the most attention, so every video opens on its busiest stretch (a storm cell, a spotlight event, fish, a gust), with music within seconds. See ambient-events "Strong opening".
- **Never commit renders or big binaries.** Outputs, caches and model weights go in gitignored folders (`out/`, `cache/`, `.venv/`). Key-art PNGs in `assets/keyart/` *are* committed (they're source).
- **The repo goes public as a snapshot (user 2026-10-26, 2026-10-27).** This repo stays private. The public repo, [TrumanBrown/cozy-vibe-vids-public](https://github.com/TrumanBrown/cozy-vibe-vids-public), is a snapshot of main made by `tools/go_public.py` (to update it: go_public into `C:\dev\cozy-vibe-vids-public`, its clone, then push that folder): it never carries this repo's history, and it leaves out the private notes, which are the chat archives in `docs/history/`, `docs/STATUS.md`, `docs/DECISIONS.md`, the new-video lessons and retrospective, and `marketing/`. Everything else is published, so write it without quoting Truman's messages (say what he asked in your own words, never a `**User:**` line; `tests/test_go_public.py` and go_public fail on six of his words in a row from the chat archives or the lessons), and never put a personal email, a user folder (`C:\Users\<name>`: write `%USERPROFILE%` or a repo path), Azure subscription or tenant IDs, keys or tokens in any tracked file or commit message. The pre-commit hook (`tools/secret_scan.py`, on with `git config core.hooksPath .githooks`) refuses the identifiers; the private terms it looks for live in `.git/info/private-terms`, never in the repo. Commits use the GitHub noreply identity (repo config). Never make this repo public, and never `git push --mirror` from here (it would publish `refs/agents/*`).
- **Long renders** are segmented, resumable, run in the background, and report progress/ETA.
- **A fresh chat session at each stop.** Every image an agent views is stored in its session log. The 2026-10-17 to 20 session ("Automating aesthetic video generation") grew to 900 MB, 757 MB of it images, and VS Code can no longer open it (`RangeError: Invalid string length`); the Treetop `/kickoff` session broke the same way at 612 MB on 2026-10-16. Start a new session at each stop of a video (key-art pick, sample, music, upload); STATUS.md, DECISIONS.md and the lessons carry the context. Before a session that won't open is deleted, archive its checkpoints, Truman's messages and its todo list in [docs/history/](docs/history/) and check every request he made against the lessons (both were, 2026-10-20).
- **Cross-platform tooling.** Desktop = native Windows (PowerShell); laptop = WSL. Scripts are Node/Python with path APIs.
- **Code changes keep the safety net green (2026-10-26).** Run `tests/` before and after any change to `tools/`, `src/` or `scenes/` (free, offline, about 11 s). Refactors follow [TASKS.md](TASKS.md), one task per commit, tool names and flags unchanged.
- **Record decisions** (dated, with the why) in [docs/DECISIONS.md](docs/DECISIONS.md); keep README, SETUP and the commands below current. Commit at milestones; push to `origin`.

## Commands

The full list with every flag: [.agents/skills/new-video/references/commands.md](.agents/skills/new-video/references/commands.md). The everyday ones:

```powershell
# a new video from one line (new-video skill, Autopilot): gates checks, review, critic; one stop for his OK
.venv\Scripts\python tools\autopilot.py run "<idea>"            # resume: run --scene <scene> --from <stage>; status <scene>
.venv\Scripts\python tools\finish_video.py <scene>              # after his OK: 20 min loop window, 10 h with music, thumbnails, upload folder
.venv\Scripts\python tools\footage_loop.py cut|analyze|seam|heroes|window|master <scene>   # his own recording looped to 10 h (scenes/<scene>/footage.json; the aquarium, 2026-10-22)
.venv\Scripts\python tools\color_edit.py <scene>                # his color editor in the browser (iPhone's sliders; Save look -> footage.json "grade", then a new window --tag and master)
.venv\Scripts\python tools\review.py <scene> [--render out\<scene>\render\<tag>]   # every check from the lessons; must PASS before he sees anything
.venv\Scripts\python tools\critic.py <scene> --render out\<scene>\render\<tag>     # the eye checks, judged by the vision model
node src/cli.mjs still   scenes/<scene> [--frames 0,900] [--params debug=rain]
node src/cli.mjs preview scenes/<scene> [--seconds 10] [--start <frame> | --event lightning:0]
node src/cli.mjs render  scenes/<scene> [--from 20m --length 20m] [--name tag] [--music auto|off]
.venv\Scripts\python tools\loop_master.py out\<scene>\render\loop20_<v> --times 30 --name 10h_<v>
.venv\Scripts\python tools\upload_package.py assets\publish\<scene>.json   # -> upload/<slug>/
.venv\Scripts\python tools\new_video.py spend --since <date>    # Azure spend (out/azure_usage.jsonl)
.venv\Scripts\python -m unittest discover -s tests              # the safety net (TASKS.md T1): before and after any code change
```

- Launch Electron only via `node src/cli.mjs` (`electron.exe` is a GUI-subsystem binary, so the shell won't wait for it).
- PowerShell: pass negative-number flags as `--target=-4,1,-40`. Windows PowerShell 5 corrupts UTF-8 when piping text into Node/Python, so write script files instead.
